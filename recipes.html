Traditional Irish Soda Bread Recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (500 g) plain flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1½–2 cups buttermilk

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Sift the flour, baking soda, and salt into a large bowl.
  3. Make a well in the center and pour in most of the buttermilk.
  4. Using your hand, gently bring the dough together. Add more buttermilk if needed.
  5. Shape into a round, place on a floured tray, and cut a deep cross on top.
  6. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.
