Traditional Irish Soda Bread

Irish soda bread is a simple, honest loaf made from just a handful of ingredients: flour, baking soda, salt, and buttermilk. No yeast, no sugar, no fuss—just a beautiful, rustic bread that has nourished families for generations.

This site is dedicated to preserving the traditional recipe and sharing the story behind it: where it came from, how it was made, and why its simplicity still matters today.

What you'll find here

  • History: The origins of soda bread in Ireland.
  • Recipes: The classic loaf and a few respectful variations.
  • Tips: How to get the texture and crust just right.
